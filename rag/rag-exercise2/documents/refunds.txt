Refund Policy 1: Standard Product Refund
Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. The product must be returned in its original condition along with proof of purchase.

Refund Policy 2: Digital Product Refund
Digital products are not eligible for a refund once they have been downloaded. Customers experiencing technical problems should contact customer support.

Refund Policy 3: Subscription Refund
Customers can request a refund for an annual subscription within 14 days of subscription activation. Refunds requested after 14 days will not be accepted.

Refund Policy 4: Travel Booking Refund
Travel bookings can be cancelled for a full refund up to 48 hours before the scheduled departure. Cancellations made within 48 hours may incur a cancellation fee.

Refund Policy 5: Employee Training Refund
Training course fees can be refunded if the employee cancels at least 7 days before the training start date. No refund will be provided after the training has started.
